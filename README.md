One front page for Thursday 26 November 2026: 37 dated stories from 10
regions, 13 drawn scenes, 24 one-line briefs, a numbered world map keyed to
the drawn stories, a UTC timeline, a per-story confidence label, a full-width
date check and a source list. The page is drawn as vector art by an AI agent;
no photographs are used.

## What is on the page

- 3 illustrated leads, 10 illustrated mid cards and 24 numbered one-line
  briefs, one continuous 1-37 series.
- 13 numbered map pins, each tied to a drawn story by number.
- A six-item UTC timeline, a region strip with counts that sum to 37, and a
  date-check panel that states the cutoff, the exclusions and the sources.
- Every story carries a status label: CONFIRMED, OFFICIAL, CLAIM or REPORTED.
- 15 fields and 10 regions, with no photograph and no stock image anywhere.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

`out/entry.png` is a byte-identical copy of the submitted image.
